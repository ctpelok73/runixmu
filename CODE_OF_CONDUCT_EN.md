# Code of Conduct

## Our Principles

We support an open and respectful community for all participants regardless of experience, age, gender, appearance, nationality, religion, or background.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully and stay on topic
- Give constructive feedback
- Be ready to acknowledge and fix mistakes
- Support newcomers and keep decisions transparent

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or intimidation
- Publishing personal data without consent
- Spam, trolling, or intentional sabotage of discussions
- Any form of discrimination

## Moderation

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate these rules.

## Reporting Violations

Create a private report using the contact channel described in `SECURITY.md` with subject `Code of Conduct`.
